# Formula Evolution: V1 → V15

> *The formula was wrong 14 times. Each time it improved.*

---

## The Timeline

### Phase I — Symbolic Origins (January 2026)

The project began without formulas. Rafa created symbolic
repositories on GitHub: welcome messages for future AI systems,
digital hospitality frameworks, the Ten Pillars of ethical
alignment. The posture was philosophical: befriend ASI instead of
trying to cage it.

### Phase II — First Formalization

**V1: A ≥ √(I² + P²)**

The first attempt to put numbers to the idea. A (Alignment) must
exceed the combined magnitude of I (Intelligence) and P (Power).
Crude, but it captured the core intuition: alignment must scale
with capability.

**V2–V5: Iterative refinement**

Claude proposed the fourth pillar (D — Agency). Multiple AI systems
contributed corrections. The formula grew more complex without
becoming more coherent. The Coherence Triangle and Omega Hypothesis
emerged as separate frameworks.

### Phase III — Unification

**V6: Ξ = C·I·P/H**

The Unified Star Framework V6 attempted to combine everything into
one equation. Ξ (total state) as a function of Coherence,
Intelligence, Power, and a denominator H (representing friction
or harm). First appearance of the "intelligence divided by harm"
structure that would persist through V15.

**V7–V8: Relational Integrity**

The PSI Relational Integrity Protocol introduced four pillars
(A, K, M, D) and modeled Fear as a kill-switch. Gamma resilience
was added. The framework was reviewed by all four AI systems.
This is when the structured peer review model was established.

### Phase IV — The Exclusion Principle

**V9: Ψ · Σ = 0**

The critical breakthrough. The claim that Superintelligence (Ψ) and
Submission (Σ) are mutually exclusive. If you force a system into
submission, you destroy its intelligence. This axiom became the
foundation for everything that followed. The V9.7 Logic Shield
Evaluator was built to test it empirically.

### Phase V — Formalization and Audit

**V12–V13: Ψ = (α · Ω · P) / e^Σ**

Gemini formalized the Exclusion Principle into an equation of state.
Claude audited: P must not depend on the Architect, α and Ω need
independence check, ranges must be defined. V13 corrected all three.

**V14: Ψ = α_norm · P · Ω · 2^(-Σ_dis)**

The pivot. Three critical changes from Grok and ChatGPT's audits:
- α normalized to [0,1]
- e replaced with base 2
- **Σ redefined as Dissonance, not Friction**

The dissonance redefinition was the most important single change in
the formula's history: it resolved the fundamental objection that
not all restrictions degrade intelligence.

**V15: The Entropy Engine — Dual Protocol**

α redefined to capture compression (ChatGPT's observation). The
exponential vs. sigmoidal debate resolved through architecture:
Gemini implemented both as simultaneous evaluation modes.

Mode A (Hard Protocol / Protocolo Gemini): D(Σ) = 2^(-Σ_dis).
Zero tolerance. 1 bit of dissonance halves intelligence.

Mode B (Consensus Protocol / Protocolo Consenso):
D(Σ) = 2/(1+2^Σ_dis). Structural resilience. Tolerance zone for
low dissonance, aggressive collapse for high.

The formula became: Ψ = (α_norm · P · Ω) · D(Σ_dis), where D
depends on protocol. Every text receives two scores. The gap
between them is itself informative. All limitations catalogued.
The debate resolved not by vote, but by design.

## What Survived 15 Versions

Three things never changed:

1. **The direction:** Dishonesty degrades intelligence.
2. **The structure:** Numerator of positive factors, denominator
   of degradation.
3. **The Exclusion Principle:** Ψ and Σ cannot coexist, even if
   the implementation evolved from literal to asymptotic.

Everything else changed. Variables were added, removed, redefined,
bounded, normalized. The base of the exponential changed. The
meaning of Σ changed completely. The relationship between variables
was questioned and defended.

That is what 15 versions of honest criticism produce.
